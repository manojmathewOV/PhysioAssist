## Required record for a resolution

Record decision ID, selected source revision/digest, the actual approved wording/parameters, applicable procedure/phase, approver identity and date, superseded rule and any exceptions. Clinical approval requires a real decision from the treating authority; an agent must not mark it approved itself.

Do not average conflicting angle limits, choose the more permissive source, or copy older hold durations into a new prescription without confirmation. Publishing an approved excerpt and approving its use for a patient remain separate actions.
